Why recipes let you down

Hi, I'm Maren. I ran the ovens at a small neighbourhood bakery for eleven years, and I hear that question every week.

Most recipes give you clock times like "rise for 4 hours" or "proof overnight." But dough doesn't own a watch. It speeds up or slows down with the heat in your kitchen.

At my bakery, the first rise took 3 hours in July and 7 hours in January. Same flour, same starter, same baker. If I'd followed a 4-hour recipe in winter, half my loaves would have come out flat.

So I teach one simple rule: read the dough. Learn the few signs that show it's ready, and trust them over the timer.
